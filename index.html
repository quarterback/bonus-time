Ron Bronson

Finds messy systems. Makes them legible. Builds what’s missing. Keeps them moving.

I’m a systems designer, writer, and professor. I work on the decision layer: the systems that determine how institutions operate, coordinate, and stay accountable as AI becomes part of how the public gets served. I teach Urban Technology at the University of Michigan, build Vloora, and run public programming through my studio, Thinking Weapons. Offline I coach high school tennis and host a radio show.

Practice

Eight years at 18F, close to four of them as Head of Design, running the largest in-house design practice in the federal government. I set its headcount, approved its hiring, and built the career ladders it ran on. What I build now starts from one question: when software makes a call on someone’s behalf, who decided, and where is the record?
