Ron Bronson

Michigan hired me to help define what Urban Technology becomes as a field. I teach Public Mechanics, a graduate course on how public services actually work, and I advise State Capacity AI.

At 18F I was asked to build the infrastructure for a profession that didn’t have any yet: hiring standards, career paths, Delivery School, and one of the largest design organizations in the federal government. I kept getting pulled outside my lane, too — the FTC Chair’s office, chief of staff for TTS, the Office of Customer Experience.

I’ve been asked to start or run convenings for twenty years. Indianapolis Design Week. Design For The Public. AIGA Portland made me president during a period of organizational change, and Portland Design Month grew to fifteen hundred people.

Portland’s mayor appointed me to the Historic Landmarks Commission. Oregon’s coaches association asked me to help redesign how high school team tennis works statewide, and I wrote the proposal that did it. Different institutions, same request: build the capability that isn’t there yet, and build it to outlast you.
