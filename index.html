What I am brought in to do.

Lead complex implementation

Bring product, technology, research, communications, policy, and operations into one delivery effort. Set direction, make the tradeoffs visible, and keep the work moving.

Build delivery capability

Shape teams, management structures, hiring, standards, and working practices so an organization can keep delivering after the initial push.

Modernize services

Improve services, platforms, workflows, and internal systems from discovery through implementation, adoption, and sustainment.

Step into difficult work

Take on initiatives that are stalled, politically difficult, technically uncertain, or spread across too many owners. Clarify what matters and create a path people can act on.

Strategy through field delivery.

My career has crossed several disciplines because the work required it. The throughline is responsibility for how institutions use technology, organize delivery, and make consequential decisions.

I have led statewide digital operations, city service modernization, cross-agency initiatives, public platforms, and multidisciplinary organizations of more than 40 people.

At 18F, I advanced from hands-on delivery to leading its 40-plus-person multidisciplinary design organization, serving on the leadership team and taking executive assignments across Technology Transformation Services, the Federal Trade Commission, and GSA headquarters.
