    About Me

    Institutions keep asking me to build capability they don’t have yet. GSA asked me to lead one of the federal government’s largest design organizations. AIGA Portland’s members made me their board president. The University of Michigan hired me to teach urban technology at Taubman College, where I’m an Assistant Professor of Practice.

    More than a decade of it was federal — eight years at 18F, advising the FAS Deputy Commissioner, with teams working alongside the White House, Congress, FTC, CMS, VA and DOD. Before Washington, digital strategy in higher education and local government. Before all of it, the US Air Force.

    What I Believe

    • Systems rarely fail all at once. They leak, jam, and get stuck.
    • When they do, it isn’t designers or executives who respond. It’s the people already inside them, doing the work that keeps things running.
    • The last mile of delivery is always political.
    • Maintenance is the work. The launch is just the part with a photographer.
    • The object of design isn’t the artifact. It’s the incentives, processes, and institutions that decide what the artifact does to people.
    • Consequence is a design material. What a service does to someone is a decision, whether or not anyone meant to make it.

    Who I’d Like to Meet

    • People rebuilding institutions that already exist, rather than starting something adjacent and calling it a fix.
    • Anyone who has had to make a thirty-year-old system work on a Monday morning.
    • People who understand that procurement is a design problem.
    • Teams who want a practice built, not just a designer hired.
    • Students who would rather repair something than launch something.
