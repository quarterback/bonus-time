Design belongs where the problem is.

I am a design leader and builder with two decades across the web, institutional delivery, and technical implementation.

I built and led one of the first large-scale forward-deployed design practices. I have led departments and advised the executives I reported to.

Web, delivery, and code.

For a decade I rescued and ran institutional websites. I moved organizations from hand-coded pages to content management systems, then through the social media era.

As the web lead, I also ran marketing and strategic communications. I wrote code, trained staff, managed vendors, and kept public systems working.

Design leadership.

My design practice grew out of the web and expanded into research, service design, content, product, and communications. I later took responsibility for organizational design and executive advisory.

I wrote hiring standards, rebuilt job descriptions, ran performance reviews, and decided who staffed each engagement. I managed managers and developed designers into their next roles.

I speak globally and teach Public Mechanics. I still prototype services, write software, facilitate working sessions, and debug the systems around the work.

How services work.

The cheaper generating solutions gets, the scarcer understanding the problem becomes.

I study how services actually work. I trace where authority and judgment sit, how paperwork moves, where handoffs fail, and who absorbs the friction.

I apply that lens to forward-deployed design, trajectory management, agent experience, and public trust.

Side quests.

Offline, I am a longtime high school tennis coach who has won state titles and coach-of-the-year awards. I have served on several community nonprofit boards.

I have worked as a radio DJ and news anchor. I created sabermetrics for the Finnish sport of pesäpallo and invented a sport called Tennis Polo 22 years ago.
