      I’m a Principal at the Global Office of Public Delivery, an independent office for people working on the machinery of public life. I’m also an Assistant Professor of Practice in Urban Technology at the University of Michigan’s Taubman College.

      I spent eight years at 18F in executive and senior leadership roles, including Head of Design and Strategic Initiatives, and later served as principal senior advisor to the Deputy Commissioner of Technology Transformation Services. I established the federal government’s first dedicated service design unit and co-built the platform states use to secure billions in Medicaid funding.

      I built closedtab, a shared record for human-agent teams. I speak globally on public delivery, service design, digital statecraft, and technology’s impact on public trust.
