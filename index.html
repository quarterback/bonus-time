Product Leadership | Web Dev Coaching · Blog ·

Ron Bronson studies how technology changes markets, organizations, and public power, drawing on economic sociology, critical urbanism, and more than two decades of experience building and directing digital systems.

He is an assistant professor of practice in Urban Technology at the University of Michigan and founder of Vloora, where his work on AI agents extends a wider interest in who makes decisions, who bears their consequences, and how systems can be changed.
