Coaching

Ron Bronson enters his tenth season as a high school tennis coach, with a career varsity record of 91–33–4 across programs in three states.

He is one of Oregon’s two state tennis co-chairs, and in that role he led the redesign of the state team tournament. He also built and runs oregontennis.org, the most advanced high school tennis ranking system in the country. He has owned it for five years.

In 2026 Oregon adopted a dual team tennis championship, the first expansion of its state tournament since 1946. It debuts in spring 2027 with power-ranking-seeded brackets: 12 teams at 6A and 8 each at 5A and 4A–1A, per gender. The higher seed hosts through to a final at the state championship site, and the rankings the brackets are seeded from are his.

That system carries four years of match data and four rating models he wrote, with an analytics layer (Power Index, FWS, FWS+, TOSS) covering every classification in the state. He built it end to end.

His teams have won a state championship, four District and Regional titles, and five Conference titles. Four of his players have won individual state championships.
