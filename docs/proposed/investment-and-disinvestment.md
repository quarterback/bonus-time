# The library and the magic machine

**Format:** essay
**Inquiry:** Markets, platforms and value capture
**Worked out:** March 2026

## The argument

Writing about AI "unlocking" progress in education or civic life pulls a sleight of hand. It describes money spent on tools to do things people have tried to do for a long time, while the ordinary institutions that did that work go unfunded. Every dollar spent on the next magic machine is a choice about what society values. The internet and cheap laptops carried the same promise.

The anchor image: a class visit to a Carnegie built Detroit library branch that was closed for eighteen months during COVID and still can't get money for its branches.

This is not an argument that AI is useless. It asks what was disinvested to pay for it, and who decided.

## Builds on

The rentier critique of the AI industry; "the rails of trust"; the maintainers thesis.
