# Name the joint

**Format:** essay or talk; a methods piece
**Inquiry:** method, across all three
**Worked out:** late July 2026, from Pamela Paul's essay on sociology in *The Wall Street Journal*

## The argument

Inside the culture war version of the critique of sociology is a real point, Ilana Redstone's: treating unequal outcomes as enough evidence of a cause. That is a genuine identification problem, and it matters in applied civic work because it lets people skip the diagnosis. If you already know the answer is "structure," you never have to say which part of the structure, and that is exactly where repair happens.

The cost for practitioners is that words like "systemic" and "structural" become flagged vocabulary. An explanation of why an eligibility system produces a given error rate gets heard as ideology instead of mechanism.

The response is method: Policy Intent, Implementation Stack, Friction Gap, Power Audit. Each step requires naming the specific joint where the failure happens. That meets the empiricist demand on its own terms without giving up the analysis.
