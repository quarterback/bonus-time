# Testimony and trace

**Format:** essay for a general audience; the concept also anchors part of the classification paper
**Inquiry:** Authority, judgment and public life
**Worked out:** June to November 2026

## The argument

What an institution says about a decision is testimony. It can be reconstructed and negotiated, and it is produced for whoever asks. What the decision mechanism records at the moment it acts is trace. Every accountability requirement becomes practical at the point where it is restated as a required trace, and becomes theater wherever it can only be testified to.

Engin and colleagues write that where the chain of authorization is broken, "responsibility cannot be borne, only performed." Testimony is how responsibility gets performed.

Robodebt is the demonstration. The human review that was supposed to check automated debt notices was gradually removed. That was invisible in testimony and would have been visible in trace. Establishing it took a Royal Commission, years later, at national expense.

From the after action record corpus: timing is what keeps an account honest. A record made during or at the close of a run limits confabulation in a way no reconstruction can, because reconstruction is testimony by definition. And testimony can be checked against trace mechanically.
