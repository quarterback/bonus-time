# The wrong hero

**Format:** essay or talk
**Inquiry:** Digital statecraft and public capability
**Worked out:** across 2026; now stated on masthead as a Position

## The argument

Capacity is an amount: staff, budget, systems, something a legislature can meter up or down. Capability is whether the thing gets done for the person. The state capacity conversation centers the wrong hero. Its stories are about the bureaucrat or technologist who cut the rule, never the person downstream. It also loosens burdens selectively, removing the friction its champions dislike and keeping the friction that suits them.

The test of any reform is whether the person got the thing they needed. Faster procurement and modern stacks count when the answer is yes.
