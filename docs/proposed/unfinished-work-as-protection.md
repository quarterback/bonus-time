# Why the work stays unfinished

**Format:** essay, possibly the start of a book on how institutions run
**Inquiry:** organizations
**Worked out:** October 2026

## The argument

Staff in civil service and in large organizations often keep work unfinished as protection against being made redundant. Finished work ends the reason for the role. Incentives that reward mediocre or ongoing work, institutions with no urgency when a problem has a short window, and people making the work about themselves all follow from the same arrangement. Product metrics thinking makes it worse when it enters how organizations are run.

This extends Public Mechanics past technology into how the people who run cities and institutions organize themselves and approach the work. It also raises a harder question: whether a career civil service makes sense outside jobs like public works.
