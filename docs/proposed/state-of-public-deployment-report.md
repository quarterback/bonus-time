# State of Public Deployment

**Format:** annual report built on a practitioner survey
**Inquiry:** Digital statecraft and public capability
**Worked out:** April 2026

## The idea

Nobody publishes a serious annual report on public sector technology practice from the practitioner's side. Consultancies publish reports, the GAO publishes audits and think tanks publish policy papers. A short, opinionated report built on a survey of people inside agencies would travel, because those people are not being served.

Shape: a short foreword stating the thesis, a methods snapshot, four chapters that are arguments dressed as findings, each with survey figures and practitioner quotes, then key takeaways. Candidate chapters:

- Most public sector AI work is stuck in pilot.
- Nobody owns deployment.
- The cost of standing still is paid in public trust.
- The teams that ship have something in common.

## Model

Framer's annual report on web teams, for format and length. **Check** the exact title before citing it.

## Needs

A survey instrument, a distribution list, and a decision on whether it publishes under Ron's name, the Global Office of Public Delivery, or the university.
