# The fan as product, twice

**Format:** a short report in the style of an investment research note
**Inquiry:** Markets, platforms and value capture
**Worked out:** February 2026

## The argument

Sports have gone through successive layers of financialization, and each layer requires the next. Ownership moved from community institutions (the Green Bay model) to billionaires to private equity and sovereign wealth. Broadcast rights were split across paywalled services, which gated what used to be shared. Betting then turned the fan's attention and the fan's actions into two revenue streams. Leagues become marketing arms for sportsbooks, and rule and broadcast decisions start serving the bet.
