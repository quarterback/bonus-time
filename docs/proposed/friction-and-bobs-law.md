# Some friction is the policy

**Format:** essay; started as a public reply in October 2026
**Inquiry:** Authority, judgment and public life
**Worked out:** October 2026

## The argument

Friction is not always bad, and removing it is not always a gain. Some slogs exist for good reason and protect people. Technology companies should not be the ones who decide which frictions stay.

**Bob's Law** is the name for the gap between what a statute says and what is enforced at someone's discretion. Tools that fix a scheduling problem are useful. Tools that paper over an unresolved policy question move the decision somewhere nobody voted on it. Agents flooding government services is the live example: they deepen the advantage of people with agents, and the equity question is bigger than the queue jumping question.
