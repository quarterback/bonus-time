# Reverse underwriting

**Format:** article first, prototype screens later
**Inquiry:** Authority, judgment and public life
**Worked out:** October 2026; a draft exists and needs cleanup

## The argument

Credit scores rate people for the benefit of lenders. As AI agents act on people's behalf, the direction should reverse: people should hold a running score of the agents and AI products they use, built from what those agents actually did for them. It is underwriting done by the customer, held by the customer.

Design notes from the sessions: the scale should feel like baseball scouting grades (20 to 80) or a familiar credit range, and the scoring mechanics can borrow from pinball, where specific events add or subtract.
